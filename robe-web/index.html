How it works

Borrow it. Wear it. Buy it if you fall in love.

Rent from private wardrobes and shops, with every piece cleaned and checked before it reaches you.

  1. 1

    Browse & rent

    Find a piece, pick your dates, and check out with your deposit held on your card.

  2. 2

    It arrives, ready to wear

    Delivered to your door or a parcel locker near you, already cleaned and inspected.

  3. 3

    Wear it for 10 days

    You get a short window to accept the delivery or flag a problem before the rental clock starts.

  4. 4
    Optional

    A fresh clean, if asked

    If the owner or shop asks for it, drop it at a partner laundry near you when your 10 days are up. They clean it, check it and send it home.

  5. 5

    Return it, or buy it

    Post it back with the prepaid label, or buy it outright if you can't bear to let it go.

Why Robe

Made for borrowing, not for keeping.

  1. Cleaned and checked

    Every piece is cleaned and inspected before it reaches you.

  2. A hold, not a charge

    Most deposits are a temporary hold on your card, released within 48 hours of the item being checked in.

  3. Ten days from delivery

    The rental clock starts when you confirm delivery. Need longer? Ask the owner for ten more.

  4. Lockers round the corner

    Collect from an InPost locker or a Post Office near you, anywhere in the UK.

  5. A fresh clean, if asked

    Some owners ask for a partner laundry clean when you're done. It's arranged at checkout.

  6. Robe Rewards

    Every 3 rentals returned in good condition earn 50% off the rent of your next one.
