China Hand — Support

China Hand is a card game (Shithead / Palace) for iPhone and iPad. This page is for help, questions, or reporting a problem.

Contact: sacchi.appdev@outlook.com
I read every message and usually reply within a few days.

Common questions

  • The app crashed / a screen looks wrong. Tell me what you were doing right before it happened, and your device model + iOS version if you know it — that's the single most useful detail for tracking a bug down.
  • Local multiplayer won't find anyone. Both devices need Wi-Fi and Bluetooth on, need to be near each other, and need to have accepted the local-network permission prompt the first time the app asked for it. Check Settings → China Hand → Local Network is enabled.
  • I bought something and it didn't unlock. Email me the approximate date and what you purchased, and I'll help sort it out. Nothing is ever lost — Apple keeps a record of every purchase even if the app didn't apply it correctly.
  • I have a rules question or a house-rule suggestion. Happy to hear it — email me directly.
